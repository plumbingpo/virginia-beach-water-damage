Few water damage events are as common, or as potentially devastating to a property, as a pipe that bursts. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. The trigger may be a hard freeze, corroded or aging lines, high pressure or physical damage, but the need is the same: act immediately so water does less harm to the structure, the floors, your possessions and the building's systems.

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Outdoor faucets should be closed, drained and free of hoses ahead of freezing weather
  • Leak detectors placed close to main supply lines and the water heater give early warning
  • A plumbing inspection by a licensed pro every few years catches corrosion and wear early
  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • Monitor your water pressure, because excessive pressure (above 80 PSI) strains pipes and fittings

Signs You Need Burst Pipe Repair in Virginia Beach

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Hissing or rushing noises in the walls with every tap turned off
  • Rusty or discolored water coming from your taps
  • A hidden leak often shows up first as a much larger water bill
  • A sudden drop in water pressure throughout your property
  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • Water pooling on floors with no apparent source
  • A stained, soggy or bowed section of drywall overhead or beside you
