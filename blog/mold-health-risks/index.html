Health Effects of Mold Exposure in Virginia Beach, VA

Home / Blog / Mold Health Risks in Virginia Beach, VA

Beyond looking bad, mold poses a serious threat to health, and millions of people feel its effects each year. If you are a homeowner in Virginia Beach, VA with moisture problems or water damage, you need to understand the health risks of mold exposure before deciding on remediation. This guide from Virginia Beach Restoration covers what you need to know about mold and its effects on health.

Mold's Effects on the Body

Mold gives off irritants and allergens, and some types also make toxic substances known as mycotoxins. Inhalation, ingestion, and contact with skin are the three ways they get into the body. The severity of health effects depends on the type and concentration of mold, duration of exposure, and individual sensitivity. Typical symptoms are coughing, wheezing, a congested nose, irritated eyes and throat, headaches, and skin rashes. For those with a compromised immune system, asthma, or allergies, the reaction to mold may be worse.

Which Groups Face the Greatest Risk

Mold exposure can affect anybody, but the risk is higher for elderly people, infants and young children, anyone with allergies or an immune system disorder, people on immunosuppressive medications, and those with a respiratory condition like COPD or asthma. Getting mold remediated promptly matters most for people in these groups.
