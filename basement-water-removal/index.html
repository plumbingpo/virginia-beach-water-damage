Few problems rattle a homeowner like finding the basement under water. Heavy rain that overloads the drainage, a dead sump pump, a failed water heater, a broken pipe and foundation seepage all put water downstairs, where it has to be dealt with by professionals right away before it damages the structure, grows mold and destroys what you store there. Virginia Beach Restoration provides 24/7 emergency basement water removal throughout Virginia Beach, using powerful extraction equipment and advanced drying technology to get your basement dry and safe as quickly as possible.

Limited airflow, below-grade construction and a habit of staying damp make a basement harder to dry than any other part of the house. Moisture gets trapped by the surrounding soil, the poured floor and the block walls, so it will not evaporate naturally. Our restoration technicians understand these challenges and bring specialized equipment: submersible pumps for deep water, truck-mounted extraction units for large volumes, and dehumidifiers and air movers positioned for below-grade drying.

Beyond removing the water, we address the root cause of your basement flooding to help keep it from happening again. Our technicians inspect the sump pump system, foundation drainage, window wells, wall penetrations and grading to identify what contributed. From there you get our recommendations for lasting fixes, which may include waterproofing, interior drainage or installing a backup sump pump to guard the basement against the next intrusion.

How to Prevent Basement Water Removal Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep gutters flowing freely to stop roof water from spilling next to basement walls
  • Make sure downspouts discharge at least 6 feet away from your foundation
  • Check that the ground next to the house still slopes away, and regrade where it has settled
  • Consider an interior French drain system if seepage is a chronic issue
  • Put a water alarm near the sump pit and the water heater for early warning
  • Test your sump pump quarterly by pouring water into the pit and confirming it runs

The Benefits of Professional Basement Water Removal

Removing water from a basement calls for know-how and equipment well past the consumer fans and pumps most homeowners own. Professional submersible pumps extract water far faster than rental equipment, and our commercial drying systems create the temperature, airflow and humidity conditions needed to dry below-grade structures that naturally resist evaporation. Skip that step and the dampness remaining in insulation, concrete and wood framing will bring mold and break the materials down.

A basement under water is hazardous in ways that are easy to overlook. Electrocution is possible whenever water reaches or covers outlets, wiring, the panel or plugged-in appliances. The water heater, gas piping and furnace may be damaged too. Our technicians are trained to assess and mitigate these hazards before extraction begins, keeping your family and our crew safe throughout the restoration.

Signs It Is Time for Basement Water Removal

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Standing water on your basement floor, whatever the source
  • Strange sounds from the sump pump, constant cycling or a pump that stays silent
  • Cracks in the floor, seams between wall and floor or gaps around pipes letting water in
  • Basement windows that drip or window wells that fill during rain
  • Finished walls downstairs with bubbled paint or drywall that crumbles
  • Efflorescence, a chalky white residue, forming on masonry or concrete
  • Moisture showing low on the basement walls, close to the floor
